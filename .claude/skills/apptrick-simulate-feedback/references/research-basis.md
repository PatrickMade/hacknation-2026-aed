# Method limits

This skill produces model-generated hypotheses, not observations of real users.
A coherent persona may reflect model bias or repeat its input. Repeated answers
are not independent confirmation. Do not infer population rates, confidence
intervals, saturation or predictive accuracy from this panel.

Keep source passages and assumptions distinct. Compare against held-out real
interviews only when those notes were not used to build the personas. If the
coordinator already saw them, disclose possible contamination; do not claim a
blind evaluation. Use differences to plan real research, not to publish scores.

The imported design provenance is recorded in the repository adoption manifest.
Research claims from that design are not runtime guarantees of this workflow.
