# Quality gate

Before reporting, verify:

1. Every persona trait has a supplied source or an explicit assumption label.
2. All generated reactions are `[SYNTHETIC]`; observed browser behavior is
   separated from imagined user reactions.
3. Agreement, disagreement and one-off observations remain visible. Nothing was
   rerun or removed to force a desired conclusion.
4. Each hypothesis has a concrete real-user interview, data or usability check.
5. The approved persona/run budget was respected; independent context and
   browser execution claims match what actually ran.
6. Held-out comparisons disclose reuse or contamination. Counts are descriptive,
   never statistical evidence, saturation, validation or readiness approval.
7. Source paths, session identity, tested revisions and artifact links are correct;
   reports contain no credentials, personal interview data or private quotations
   beyond the authorized sanitized scope.

A failed item is corrected within scope or reported as incomplete. No automatic
extra panels and no handoff that treats a hypothesis as an accepted requirement.
