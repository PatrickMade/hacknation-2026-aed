# Logo, QR sticker and brand book for the campaign "Mapa Serc"

- Date: 2026-11-21
- Category: feature
- Priority signal: high - logo and slogan are 25% of the challenge score; all materials are due 2026-11-22 10:00 UTC
- Risk signal: low - static brand assets, no runtime code
- Routing: Next: apptrick-deliver "Logo, naklejka QR i księga znaku akcji Mapa Serc - brief: .ai/specs/briefs/2026-11-21-kierunek-kampanii-aed.md"

## Problem

The challenge "Misja AED - Dane na ratunek" requires a campaign name, a slogan
and a logotype that encourage AED dysponenci to share AED data on dane.gov.pl
("proste i zrozumiałe, zgodne z tematyką wyzwania, nowoczesne, kreatywne, z
potencjałem promocyjnym"), plus a brand book. The submission has none of these
yet. The campaign page (APP-44) needs the mark and colours before the brand
book exists.

## Agreed direction

- Name: **Mapa Serc**. Slogan: **Wszystkie AED pod ręką.** Chosen by the project
  author on 2026-11-21 (he wrote the slogan himself).
- Audience: the general public first. The campaign is visible publicly, so it
  must read well for an ordinary citizen and must not be narrowly targeted at
  collecting data from dysponenci. Simplicity is the main requirement.
- The same mark is used on a sticker with a QR code next to the device; the QR
  leads to the device record and to an incident report for its dysponent.
- The mark must not imitate gov.pl or dane.gov.pl branding and must sit next
  to the dane.gov.pl mark.
- Starting point at the time of the brief: mark sketches in a
  `brand-directions/` directory on the task branch (removed when the final
  assets were added). The mark variant was still open; see Outcome below.
- Sketch palette: green `#087F45`, dark green `#0B2E20`, light green `#DDF3E6`.
  Typeface in the sketches: Figtree (SIL Open Font License 1.1).
- Accepted supporting line for dysponent materials:
  "Lista AED w szufladzie nikogo nie uratuje."
- Rejected: eight earlier directions and four slogan rounds (too
  data-centred, too clever, or aimed only at officials). "Build nothing", that is reusing the
  challenge title as the campaign name, lost because it shows no creativity
  and names the team's mission rather than a campaign.
